AIRPORT ENVIRONMENT - ASSET SOURCES AND LICENSES
================================================

Most 3D models were downloaded from Sketchfab and CGTrader (free assets), imported into Blender, modified,
re-arranged and re-exported as FBX for this project. Each model remains the work of its original author and is
used under the license shown on its page. All Sketchfab licenses below are CC Attribution 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) unless noted; changes were made to the models.

MODIFICATIONS
-------------
Models were re-arranged, re-textured, merged and optimized (combined meshes, lightmap UVs, consolidated
materials). Ceiling, runway, stadium and vehicle lights, sirens, fog, lightmaps and light probes were created
for this project. Scripts under Assets/Scripts and Assets/Editor were written for this project.
